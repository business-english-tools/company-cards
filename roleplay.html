Role-play: at a trade fair

Be a company. Meet new business partners.

  1. Choose a company below, or tap the button.
  2. Read your facts. Practise: "My company is called ..."
  3. Stand up. Meet 3 partners. Ask and answer.
  4. Report back: "I met ... from ... They ..."

Your company:

Example conversation

AHello! I'm Linh from Blue Ocean Seafood. Nice to meet you.
BNice to meet you too, Linh. I'm Tom from Highland Tea. What does your company do?
AWe export shrimp to Korea and Japan.
BInteresting! Where is your head office?
AIt's in Nha Trang, in Vietnam.
BWhen was your company set up?
AIt was set up in 2006.
BHow many people work for you?
AWe have 640 employees. And what about your company?
BWe import tea from Vietnam. Our head office is in Edinburgh.
AGreat! Here's my card. Let's keep in touch.
BThank you. Nice talking to you!

Useful phrases

Start

  • Hello! I'm ... from ...
  • Nice to meet you.

Ask

  • What does your company do?
  • Where is your head office?
  • When was your company set up?
  • How many people work for you?
  • Do you export or import?

Finish

  • Here's my card.
  • Let's keep in touch.
  • Nice talking to you!
